Presented original research on estimating the cumulative distribution function (CDF) for sensitive quantitative variables using the Item Sum Technique (IST) and Item Sum Double List Technique (ISDLT). The presentation covered Monte Carlo simulation design, bias correction for social desirability effects, and performance comparisons between indirect and direct CDF estimators across varying sample sizes and correlation structures.

**Hosted by:** National Institute of Statistical Sciences (NISS) — New Researchers Network  
**Conference Chair:** Dr. Sharmistha Guha, Department of Statistics, Texas A&M University
