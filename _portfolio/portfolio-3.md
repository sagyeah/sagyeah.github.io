### Overview

This academic project investigates body composition data from 436 individuals, with body fat percentage as the target variable. Linear Regression, Ridge Regression, and Lasso Regression were developed and compared to examine predictive performance and the effects of regularization.

The analysis used a training and test split along with cross-validation to select regularization parameters for Ridge and Lasso. The project explores how demographic characteristics and body measurements relate to body fat percentage while demonstrating the trade-offs between traditional linear regression and penalized regression approaches.
