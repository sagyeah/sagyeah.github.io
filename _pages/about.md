I was born and raised in Ghana, where I developed a love for problem solving early on — whether it was breaking down math puzzles or trying to make sense of everyday challenges with numbers. That curiosity led me to study Mathematics at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana, where I earned my BSc in Mathematics. Those years built the analytical foundation I now bring to every project and dataset I work with.

I have since completed my Master’s degree in Applied Mathematics with a concentration in Statistics and Data Science at North Carolina A&T State University (NCA&T), graduating with a 3.9 GPA. This fall, I will be entering a PhD program in Data Science and Analytics — a step driven by a genuine passion for the field and a commitment to advancing what data can reveal and accomplish.

Between my degrees, I worked as a Data Analyst at Ghana Commercial Bank PLC in Accra, where I saw first-hand how data shapes real financial decisions — increasing processing efficiency by 15% using R and SQL, and helping reduce account closures by 21% through sentiment analysis.

At NCA&T, I served as a Graduate Research Assistant on an NSF-funded research initiative in the Department of Mathematics, supervised by Dr. Abeer Hasan, Associate Professor of Statistics. The project focuses on integrating Artificial Intelligence into data science and statistics education — exploring how AI tools can enhance how students learn and apply statistical reasoning. My contributions included designing and running simulation experiments, conducting literature reviews, and helping translate research findings into classroom implementations. I also served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for STAT 224, guiding undergraduates through applied statistics in R.

In June 2025, I presented original research on *Distribution Function Estimation for Sensitive Quantitative Variables* at the NISS New Researchers Network Virtual Conference — a national platform hosted by the National Institute of Statistical Sciences for early-career researchers.

What drives me is clarity and fairness in data-driven decisions. I am passionate about using machine learning, simulation, and explainable AI to solve real problems in finance, healthcare, and education. I am actively seeking data science internships, research collaborations, and industry opportunities where rigorous statistical thinking meets meaningful impact.

Outside of work and research, I enjoy football, content creation, and music — reminders that creativity, discipline, and human connection matter just as much as technical skill.
