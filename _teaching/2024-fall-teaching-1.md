### Overview

Served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for MATH 224, Introduction to Statistics, supporting undergraduate instruction in foundational statistical concepts and quantitative reasoning. My role focused on helping students develop a strong conceptual understanding of statistics, strengthen their problem solving skills, and confidently apply statistical reasoning to real world problems.

### Responsibilities

- Assisted with instruction in descriptive statistics, probability, probability distributions, sampling, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, correlation, regression, and other foundational statistical concepts.

- Guided students through statistical problems and classroom exercises, helping them understand both the procedures used and the reasoning behind each method.

- Provided individual and small group academic support to students who needed additional clarification on course concepts, assignments, and practice problems.

- Helped students interpret statistical results and communicate conclusions clearly within the context of real world problems.

- Assisted in developing and reviewing course materials, exercises, assignments, and assessments aligned with course learning objectives.

- Graded assignments, quizzes, examinations, and other assessments while providing feedback to help students identify mistakes and improve their understanding.

- Supported students in preparing for examinations by reinforcing key concepts, reviewing problem solving approaches, and addressing common areas of difficulty.

- Worked collaboratively with the course instructor and other teaching assistants to maintain consistent grading standards and provide effective instructional support.

- Helped identify recurring misconceptions and areas of difficulty among students and provided additional explanations and examples to strengthen understanding.

- Encouraged students to move beyond memorizing formulas by developing the statistical reasoning needed to select appropriate methods, interpret results, and justify conclusions.

### Skills Emphasized

- Statistical reasoning
- Probability and inferential statistics
- Quantitative problem solving
- Interpretation of statistical results
- Student mentoring and academic support
- Assessment and evaluation
- Written and verbal communication
- Collaborative teaching
- Critical thinking
