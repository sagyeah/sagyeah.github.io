### Overview

Served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for STAT 224, Introduction to Statistics, providing hands on instruction and support in R programming, statistical computing, and applied data analysis since Fall 2024. My responsibilities include leading computer lab sections, grading weekly reading and computing assignments, and supporting students through data analysis projects.

### Responsibilities

- Led computer lab sections focused on applying statistical concepts using R.

- Provided hands on instruction in R programming, data manipulation, visualization, statistical analysis, and interpretation of results.

- Graded weekly reading assignments and R based computing lab assignments, providing feedback on statistical reasoning, code implementation, and interpretation.

- Evaluated student data analysis projects, including the appropriate use of statistical methods, analysis, interpretation, and communication of results.

- Supported students during computer lab sessions by helping troubleshoot R code, identify errors, and understand the statistical concepts underlying their analyses.

- Provided individual support to students working through programming challenges, statistical analyses, and course assignments.

- Guided students in connecting theoretical concepts from lectures with their practical implementation in R.

- Worked with the instructional team to maintain consistent grading standards across assignments, computer labs, and data analysis projects.

### Tools & Skills

- R programming
- Statistical computing
- Data analysis
- Data visualization
- R debugging and troubleshooting
- Statistical interpretation
- Assignment and project evaluation
- Computer lab instruction
- Student mentoring and technical support
